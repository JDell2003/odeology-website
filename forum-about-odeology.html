How it works

One intake, then everything branches from the same target.

The point of Odeology is not to hand you disconnected widgets. The calorie target informs the macros. The macros inform the grocery build. The grocery plan feeds the overview and restock tracker. The training side can then compare your saved nutrition setup against the workload you are following.

01

Enter your goal and body data

Weight, body stats, goal direction, and training style are used to build a daily calorie target that is not generic.

02

Unlock macros and groceries

Protein, carbs, and fats are calculated from your target, then used to generate a grocery list, portions, and cost-focused meal planning.

03

Run training from the same system

The training flow can use your saved plan, highlight mismatches, and direct you into the next workout, restock task, or overview checkpoint.

The macro calculator is the front door. It is where calories, protein, carbs, and fats get set for the actual goal instead of guesswork.

Macro Calculator

What the macro calculator does.

Odeology's macro calculator is not just a number box. It takes your goal, training style, and body stats, then creates a daily calorie target plus a macro split for protein, carbs, and fats. That target becomes the base layer for everything else on the platform.

  • Personalized calorie targetDaily calories are aligned to whether the user is trying to lose fat, maintain, or push progress in training.
  • Macro breakdownProtein, carbs, and fats are shown as usable daily targets, not vague "eat clean" advice.
  • Goal handoff into groceriesOnce macros are set, the user can move directly into a grocery list and meal build that matches those targets.
  • Free entry pointThe product pushes the idea clearly: users can start with the calculator for free and keep moving forward from there.

Nutrition System

What happens after macros are set.

After the macro calculator, Odeology converts the target into practical planning. The user can build a grocery list, see cards or expanded views, compare month windows, and track restock timing instead of starting over every week.

  • Low-cost grocery planningThe system is built around making the nutrition plan feel buyable, not aspirational.
  • Multiple viewing modesUsers can move between grocery cards, full grocery views, meal cards, list-style meal displays, and PDF-style outputs.
  • Restock alertsThe overview side tracks what needs to be picked up again over the next 7, 14, and 30 days.
  • Saved plan behaviorBecause the plan can be tied to the account, the training side can check whether the saved food setup still matches the current goal.

Training

What the training side does.

The training flow exists to move users from planning into execution. It gives them a free program entry point, organizes training days, and uses the saved nutrition setup as context instead of pretending food and training are separate problems.

  • Free training-plan entryThe site explicitly positions training as free to start, with a "build plan" path connected to the rest of the system.
  • Today's workout and weekly viewThe overview page can surface the user's latest session, recent training window, and direct link into today's workout.
  • Nutrition alignment checksThe training flow can flag when the saved meal plan does not match the workload or goal and recommend an adjustment.
  • Workout database and portal linksThe wider training area includes a program page, workout database access, and a dashboard-style path for continuing execution.

Coaches And Chefs

Support is not just digital. Trainers guide the work, and chefs can handle the meals.

Odeology is meant to connect users to more than targets on a screen. Trainers or coaches help with the execution side of the plan: programming, consistency, and adjustments. Chefs cover the food side for people who do not want to shop, prep, and cook every meal themselves.

  • Coaches and trainersThey help users follow the right plan, stay consistent, and make changes when training, recovery, or progress starts to slip.
  • Available chefsFor users who want less friction, the chef side can take the meal plan and turn it into actual prepared food instead of another task on the list.
  • Same system, less guessworkThe point is that the macros, groceries, training, and human support all point back to the same goal instead of competing with each other.
