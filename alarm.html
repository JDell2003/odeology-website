Set your wake-up time. Keep this page open and plugged in overnight.

Hard mode

No stop button. To kill the alarm you must read the wake mission out loud (or type it if your browser has no mic support).

Turning hard mode on works any time. Turning it off only works inside your chosen window — no 2 AM negotiating with yourself.

How sleep gets logged

Arming the alarm marks lights-out. Killing it marks wake-up — that window is saved as your sleep for the night. Wake clean (no snooze) for +5 consistency points. Snooze and you get nothing.
