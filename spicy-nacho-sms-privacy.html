SMS Privacy Policy

The Spicy Nacho · 20555 Plank Rd, Zachary, LA 70791 · Last updated 30 August 2026

This policy explains what we do with the mobile phone number you give us when you join The Spicy Nacho text message program. It applies to that program specifically.

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your mobile phone number, or the fact that you consented to receive text messages, with any third party for that third party's own marketing purposes. Not now, and not ever.

1. What we collect

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • Your name, if you give it to us at the register.
  • The date you joined and how you joined (by text, or in person at the register).
  • Whether a message we sent you was delivered, and whether you replied STOP.

We do not collect the contents of your other messages, your location, or anything from your device.

2. Why we collect it

To send you the text messages you asked for: weekly specials, new menu items, hours, and reminders that you can order online. That is the whole purpose. We do not use your number for any other marketing.

3. How you gave consent

Either you texted our number (225) 422-1564 from your own handset, or you entered your own mobile number into the payment terminal at our counter to join our loyalty program and were told you would receive occasional promotional texts. Consent is never a condition of any purchase.

4. Who we share it with

Only the service providers that make the messages work, and only so they can deliver them on our behalf:

  • Twilio Inc. — our messaging provider, which transmits the messages.
  • Block, Inc. (Square) — our point-of-sale and loyalty provider, where in-person sign-ups are recorded.

These providers are contractually restricted to acting on our instructions and may not use your number for their own marketing. We may also disclose information where we are legally required to.

5. How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe, or until the program ends. When you reply STOP we keep a record that you opted out — that record is what stops us texting you again.

6. Your choices

  • Stop the messages: reply STOP to any message. This takes effect immediately.
  • Get help: reply HELP, or call (470) 602-4041.
  • Have your number deleted: call (470) 602-4041 or ask at the counter, and we will remove it from our list.

7. Cost and delivery

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies and is about one message per week. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

8. Children

This program is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly enroll them.

9. Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with a revised date. Questions about this policy:
