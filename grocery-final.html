How It Works

How we estimate grocery prices

We use your ZIP code to place you in the right U.S. region, then compare that region against our Augusta, Georgia grocery baseline.

  • We match your 5-digit ZIP code to its state using a nationwide ZIP lookup.
  • We apply an official regional price multiplier using BEA Regional Price Parities.
  • Georgia stays closest to baseline, and other areas adjust up or down from there.
  • No ZIP means we fall back to a general national estimate.
